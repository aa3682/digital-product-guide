## How figures are handled

The body pages on this site never print a limit, threshold, rate, or deadline set by law, regulation, or an agency. Those numbers change, and a figure printed in the middle of a paragraph goes stale without anyone noticing. Instead, a page names the concept in plain words and links to the one page that holds the number.

That page is [This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures). Every figure there sits in its own row with the year it applies to and a link to the primary source it was taken from. The page is checked against its sources once a year.

## How sources are chosen

When a page needs support for a claim, it cites a public primary source rather than a secondary summary: statutes and regulations, agency publications, standards bodies, and academic papers cited by their DOI. Commentary, marketing material, and other people's guides are not used as sources. Where a page rests on judgment rather than a source, it says so.

## Corrections

If you find an error, a broken link, or a figure that is out of date, report it on the repository's [Issues page](https://github.com/aa3682/guide-template/issues). Say which page and which line, and if you can, link the source that shows the correct fact.
