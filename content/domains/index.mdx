# The Business Side

Selling a digital product on your own puts you inside a set of rules that do not care which step you are on. This section covers those rules in seven areas, each written for a first-time seller and marked where it speaks to someone who already sells.

## Why these areas

The Launch Path tells you what to do and in what order. The Business Side covers what follows you from step to step: the rules you have to meet and the obligations that keep running after launch day. Grouping that material into areas lets you find one topic without reading the whole guide, and lets a seller who already has a product check one area at a time.

## The areas

- [Business Setup](/domains/business-setup): How to decide what kind of business you are running and what that choice means for you.
- [Taxes and Records](/domains/taxes-and-records): What you owe, what you keep track of, and how records make both easier.
- [Copyright and Licensing](/domains/copyright-and-licensing): What you own in the product you made, and what you can and cannot use from others.
- [Terms, Refunds, and Privacy](/domains/terms-refunds-and-privacy): What you promise buyers, how refunds work, and what you owe people whose information you collect.
- [Payments and Chargebacks](/domains/payments-and-chargebacks): How money moves from a buyer to you, and what happens when a buyer disputes a charge.
- [Email and Audience Rules](/domains/email-and-audience-rules): What the rules say about the messages you send and the list you build.
- [Marketing Claims and Disclosures](/domains/marketing-claims-and-disclosures): What you can say about your product and results, and what you have to disclose when you say it.
