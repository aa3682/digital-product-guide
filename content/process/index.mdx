# The Launch Path

Launching and selling a digital product on your own follows a repeatable sequence rather than a string of one-off decisions. This section walks through that sequence in six steps, with one page for each.

## Why a sequence

Decisions made out of order work against each other. A price set before the product exists, or a shop built before the price is known, gets redone later at a cost. A fixed order makes sure nothing is skipped, and each step hands the next one exactly what it needs.

## The steps

1. [Choose the Product](/process/choose-the-product): A decision about what to make, for whom, and why they would pay for it.
2. [Make the Product](/process/make-the-product): A finished product that is yours to sell and ready to deliver.
3. [Set the Price](/process/set-the-price): A price, and the reasoning behind it, that you can defend to yourself and explain to a buyer.
4. [Set Up the Shop](/process/set-up-the-shop): A working storefront, including file delivery, access, and customer support setup.
5. [Launch](/process/launch): A launch plan carried out, with the first sales and what you learn from them.
6. [Keep Selling](/process/keep-selling): A routine that keeps the product selling and tells you when to change something.

## A loop, not a line

Keep Selling feeds back into Choose the Product. The second product starts from what the first one taught you: who bought, what they asked for, and what you would do differently. The path is a loop you walk more than once.
