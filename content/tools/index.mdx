# Tools

This section holds the calculators, checklists, and worksheets that turn The Launch Path into something you can do at a desk. Each tool supports a specific step and explains what to do with the result.

## What belongs here

A calculator does the arithmetic behind a decision. A checklist makes sure nothing is skipped when a step has many parts. A worksheet gives you a structure for gathering facts, so what you carry into the next step is complete and consistent.

## What's here

**[This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures)** lists every limit, rate, threshold, deadline, and fee the guide refers to, with the year each applies to and its primary source, and is refreshed once a year. It is the only page on the site that names a platform, and only as the source of that platform's own fee.
