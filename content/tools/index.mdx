# Tools

This section holds the calculators, checklists, and worksheets that turn [PROCESS NAME] into something you can do at a desk. Each tool supports a specific step and explains what to do with the result.

## What belongs here

A calculator does the arithmetic behind a decision. A checklist makes sure nothing is skipped when a step has many parts. A worksheet gives you a structure for gathering facts, so what you carry into the next step is complete and consistent.
