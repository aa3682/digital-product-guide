# Tools

This section holds the reference page and the worksheets that turn The Launch Path into something you can do at a desk. Each worksheet belongs to one step, gathers the facts that step needs, and points to the rules that apply there.

## The reference page

**[This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures)** lists every limit, rate, threshold, deadline, and fee the guide refers to, with the year each applies to and its primary source, and is refreshed once a year. It is the only page on the site that names a platform, and only as the source of that platform's own fee. The worksheets never print a figure; they link to this page where one comes up.

## The worksheets

One for each step of The Launch Path, in order. Print one, or copy it into your own notes, and fill it in as you work through the step.

- **[Product Choice Worksheet](/tools/product-choice-worksheet)**, for Choose the Product: questions that turn what you already explain to people into one product with an audience, a format, and a promise you can test.
- **[Build Checklist](/tools/build-checklist)**, for Make the Product: the tasks that take the promise to a finished file you are allowed to sell and a buyer can open.
- **[Pricing Worksheet](/tools/pricing-worksheet)**, for Set the Price: questions that lead from costs and the buyer's alternative to one round price, what it leaves you, and the rules for discounting it.
- **[Shop Setup Checklist](/tools/shop-setup-checklist)**, for Set Up the Shop: the tasks behind the link, from delivery and support to the terms, the refund policy, the privacy notice, and the money.
- **[Launch Checklist](/tools/launch-checklist)**, for Launch: the tasks for the weeks before, launch day, and the weeks after.
- **[Quarterly Review](/tools/quarterly-review)**, for Keep Selling: questions to answer once a quarter about sales, refunds, the list, records, tax dates, and whether every claim is still true.
