# Launch Checklist

This checklist belongs to [Launch](/process/launch), the fifth step of The Launch Path. It runs from the weeks before the announcement, through launch day, to the first weeks of watching what happens when people who are not your friends try to buy. A first launch is a test with money in it, and the checklist keeps it honest.

## How to use it

Print it or copy it into your own notes and check each item off when it is done. Every number you write is your own: the date, the people told, the sales, the refunds. Any number set by law, an agency, or a platform lives on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures), and this page links to it where one comes up.

## The weeks before

- [ ] Pick a launch day when you can watch the shop and answer email.
- [ ] Write one message with five parts: the promise, what is inside, the price, the refund promise, and the link.
- [ ] Open the message with the words people used at the promise test.
- [ ] Remove every result you cannot back up, such as what buyers will earn or save.
- [ ] Remove any name or quote you do not have consent to use, test readers included.
- [ ] Decide on a [launch discount](/glossary#launch-discount) or none; if one, write its reason in a sentence and its end date.
- [ ] List who will be told, in order: promise-test and test-read people by name, then contacts who match the reader, then the places you already talk to people.
- [ ] Confirm that everyone on the email list agreed to hear from you, and remove anyone who did not.
- [ ] Add the required footer to the message: your postal address and a working way to [opt out](/glossary#opt-out).
- [ ] Buy the product yourself once more and follow the delivery through.

Example: Priya's message opens with the sentence two supervisors used, "I never know which forms have to be done before day one."

## Launch day

- [ ] Send the message to the first group, by name, one at a time.
- [ ] Send to the rest of the list in the order you wrote down.
- [ ] Post where you already talk to people, with the same five parts.
- [ ] Check the shop after the first sale: did the buyer receive the file or get in?
- [ ] Answer every question the same day, and write each one down in the asker's words.
- [ ] Refund anyone who asks, fast and without argument.

Example: Priya's message names the ten-page checklist, the one form, forty dollars, the two-week refund, and the link.

## The weeks after

- [ ] Fix any stall in the buying flow the day it appears.
- [ ] Keep the list of questions, refund reasons, and repeated requests going; it is the next product's demand check.
- [ ] End the discount on the date you said, at full price, with no extension.
- [ ] Honor every opt-out promptly; the window the law allows is on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#email-and-audience-rules).
- [ ] Watch the first [payout](/glossary#payout) arrive in the separate account, and note any hold; the payout schedule is set by your processor's terms, listed on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#payments-and-chargebacks).
- [ ] Write down what the first weeks showed: how many bought, who they were, what they asked.
- [ ] Read the count as proof, not income; the question was whether the promise sells to strangers.

Example: Priya writes to forty people over the week; nine buy, one asks for a refund, and three ask the same question about a state form.

## Business Side

- [Terms, Refunds, and Privacy](/domains/terms-refunds-and-privacy): The refund promise in the launch message is the one in the terms, and the two must match word for word.
- [Payments and Chargebacks](/domains/payments-and-chargebacks): The first payouts, any hold, and any early dispute arrive in these weeks, and the payout schedule and dispute fee your processor sets are on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#payments-and-chargebacks).
- [Email and Audience Rules](/domains/email-and-audience-rules): Each launch email is a [commercial message](/glossary#commercial-message) that must carry the required footer and a way to opt out, honored within the window on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#email-and-audience-rules).
- [Marketing Claims and Disclosures](/domains/marketing-claims-and-disclosures): Every sentence in the launch message is a claim, and the discount and its deadline are claims too.
