# Quarterly Review

This review belongs to [Keep Selling](/process/keep-selling), the sixth step of The Launch Path. Answer it once a quarter, on a date you put in the calendar, so the product keeps selling while you carry the obligations each sale created. Its answers feed the next product, which starts again at Choose the Product.

## How to use it

Print it or copy the questions into your own notes and answer them each quarter, keeping the last set beside the new one. Every number you write is your own: sales, refunds, subscribers, dates. Any number set by law, an agency, or a platform lives on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures), and this page links to it where one comes up rather than printing it.

## Sales

1. How many sales this quarter, and how does that compare to last quarter?
2. Where did buyers come from: the list, a public answer, a recommendation, a search?
3. Does the sales page still match the file, and does an old link still work?
4. What did buyers ask this quarter, in their words, and which question came up more than once?
5. Is a new [version](/glossary#version) due on the [cadence](/glossary#cadence) you set, and have past buyers whose price included updates been told?

Example: Priya's quarter shows thirty sales, most of them from the public page she wrote answering the state-form question.

## Refunds and disputes

1. How many refunds this quarter, and what reason did each buyer give?
2. Was every refund issued inside the window you promised?
3. Did any [chargeback](/glossary#chargeback) arrive, was it answered by its deadline with the delivery record, and what did it cost?
4. What share of this quarter's sales was disputed, and is that share rising? The level at which your processor acts is set in its terms, listed on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#payments-and-chargebacks).

## List health

1. How many people are on the list now, and how many joined or left this quarter?
2. Did every message go only to people who agreed to hear from you?
3. Did every message carry your postal address and a working way to [opt out](/glossary#opt-out)?
4. Was every opt-out honored within the window the law sets, on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#email-and-audience-rules), and is the [suppression list](/glossary#suppression-list) shared across every tool that sends?
5. When did you last write, and did you have something new to say?

Example: Priya wrote to her list once, to send the updated checklist, and three people opted out.

## Records

1. Does the ledger show every sale, refund, and expense for the quarter, with a date and an amount?
2. Does it reconcile to the processor's payout statements?
3. Are payouts still landing in the account kept apart from personal money?
4. Has every update to the product added its borrowed pieces to the rights list?
5. Is the entity's annual report, a license renewal, or an employer disclosure due this quarter? The filing offices vary by state and are pointed to on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#business-setup).
6. Are you keeping records as long as the rules require? The periods are on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#taxes-and-records).

## Estimated-tax dates

No one withholds tax from a sale, so [estimated tax](/glossary#estimated-tax) is paid through the year on set dates once what you will owe passes a threshold. Both the threshold and the dates are on [this year's figures under Taxes and Records](/tools/this-years-figures#taxes-and-records); this page prints neither.

1. Which estimated-tax date falls in the coming quarter?
2. Does the quarter's net profit change what you expect to owe for the year?
3. Are you paying by estimated payments, by raising withholding at work, or not yet at all, and is that still the right choice?
4. Has your sales tax picture changed: sales into a new state passing its threshold, or the venue changing what it collects for you? Those thresholds vary by state and are pointed to on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#taxes-and-records).

## Claims still true

1. Is every sentence on the sales page still true of the current file?
2. Does the terms page describe the product as it is now, and the refund promise as you actually keep it?
3. Has any testimonial or review appeared, and does each reflect a real buyer's honest experience?
4. Does anyone now earn a commission or receive a free copy for recommending the product, and is that [material connection](/glossary#material-connection) disclosed where they recommend it?
5. Is it time to [retire](/glossary#retire) the product, and if so, do buyers keep what they bought?

## Business Side

- [Business Setup](/domains/business-setup): An entity's annual report, a license renewal, and an employer disclosure recur while the product sells, and the offices that set the fees are on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#business-setup).
- [Taxes and Records](/domains/taxes-and-records): Estimated payments, filings, and the yearly reconciliation run on dates that arrive regardless of sales, and the dates are on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#taxes-and-records).
- [Copyright and Licensing](/domains/copyright-and-licensing): Each update adds to the rights list, and copies surface once a product sells; the registration a court case needs has a fee and a window on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#copyright-and-licensing).
- [Terms, Refunds, and Privacy](/domains/terms-refunds-and-privacy): The terms are kept matched to the product as it changes, and a retirement changes what they promise.
- [Payments and Chargebacks](/domains/payments-and-chargebacks): Reconciling payouts, keeping delivery records, and watching the dispute ratio run as long as the product sells, and the ratio your processor acts on is on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#payments-and-chargebacks).
- [Email and Audience Rules](/domains/email-and-audience-rules): The list is kept, opt-outs are honored within the window on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#email-and-audience-rules), and the suppression list is shared across every tool that sends.
- [Marketing Claims and Disclosures](/domains/marketing-claims-and-disclosures): Testimonials, reviews, and affiliates arrive here, each with a disclosure obligation, and the civil penalty a fake or undisclosed review can draw is on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#marketing-claims-and-disclosures).
