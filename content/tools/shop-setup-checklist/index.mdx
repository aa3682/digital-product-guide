# Shop Setup Checklist

This checklist belongs to [Set Up the Shop](/process/set-up-the-shop), the fourth step of The Launch Path. It covers everything behind the link the launch will send people to: how the product arrives, how a buyer gets help, the pages they read, and where the money goes. The last item is buying the product yourself.

## How to use it

Print it or copy it into your own notes and check items off as each one is done. Every number you write is your own: the refund window, the reply time, the days a link stays live. Any number set by law, an agency, or a platform lives on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures), and this page links to it where one comes up.

## Delivery

- [ ] Choose the venue you can have running this week, a [marketplace](/glossary#marketplace) or a [storefront](/glossary#storefront).
- [ ] Upload the final file, named with its version number.
- [ ] Confirm what the buyer receives after paying: a link on the confirmation page, an email, or both.
- [ ] Write down how long the link works and what a buyer does if it stops.

Example: Priya's file is delivered on the confirmation page and again by email, so a buyer who closes the tab too soon still has it.

## Access

For a course or membership, the buyer enters a space you keep running.

- [ ] Confirm how a new buyer gets in, and how soon after paying.
- [ ] Decide what happens to access when a membership ends or a buyer cancels.
- [ ] Check that the first complete set of material is in place before the first sale.

## Support

- [ ] Set up one email address for buyers, and decide when you check it.
- [ ] Choose a reply window you can keep alongside a job, and write it on the page.
- [ ] Write a short page answering the questions test readers asked.
- [ ] Confirm you can issue a refund in minutes, from your phone.

## Terms

The [terms of sale](/glossary#terms-of-sale) are the contract, strongest when shown before payment and accepted by a click.

- [ ] Start from the venue's template terms, and read every line, because the promises in them become yours.
- [ ] Add the [license](/glossary#license) sentence from Make the Product: what the buyer may and may not do.
- [ ] Add what the price includes: updates, questions, and for a membership, the period and what canceling means.
- [ ] Add a plain line saying the product is educational and results are not guaranteed.
- [ ] Link the terms from checkout, where the buyer accepts them, and from the footer.

## Refund policy

- [ ] Write the [refund policy](/glossary#refund-policy) in the same words as the promise decided in Set the Price.
- [ ] Show it before purchase, on the sales page and in the terms.
- [ ] Note how a buyer asks for a refund and how quickly you will issue it.
- [ ] For a membership, spell out the notice you give before renewal and how a buyer cancels; state rules on this vary, and [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#terms-refunds-and-privacy) points to where to look.

Example: Priya's policy is a full refund on request within two weeks, in the same words on the page and in the terms.

## Privacy notice

The [privacy notice](/glossary#privacy-notice) tells buyers what you collect and what happens to it.

- [ ] List what the shop collects: name, email address, purchase history, and whatever the analytics record.
- [ ] Name which services receive it: the payment processor, the storefront, the email service, and analytics.
- [ ] Say how long it is kept and how a buyer can ask about it.
- [ ] State that card numbers are held by the payment processor, never by you.
- [ ] Link the notice from checkout and from the footer.
- [ ] Check whether the product is directed at children, because a separate set of rules starts at the age on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#terms-refunds-and-privacy).

## Payment setup

- [ ] Open the [payment processor](/glossary#payment-processor) and storefront accounts in the right name: your own, or the entity's once one exists.
- [ ] Send every [payout](/glossary#payout) to a bank account kept apart from personal money.
- [ ] Set the sales tax setting: whether the venue collects and passes it on, or leaves it to you.
- [ ] Read the venue's published fee page and write down what it keeps from one sale; the rows for those cuts are on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#payments-and-chargebacks).
- [ ] Build the email signup form, and write beside it exactly what a subscriber is agreeing to receive.
- [ ] Buy the product yourself, on a phone, with a real card; open the file or enter the space; then refund yourself.

Example: Priya's test purchase opens a preview her phone cannot read, and she fixes the file type before a buyer sees it.

## Business Side

- [Business Setup](/domains/business-setup): The name on the storefront and processor accounts, and the account payouts go to, are your entity decision applied, and the filings it needs are pointed to on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#business-setup).
- [Taxes and Records](/domains/taxes-and-records): The payout account and the sales tax setting are where your records begin, and whether your state taxes digital products is pointed to on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#taxes-and-records).
- [Copyright and Licensing](/domains/copyright-and-licensing): The license you chose goes into the terms of sale the buyer accepts here.
- [Terms, Refunds, and Privacy](/domains/terms-refunds-and-privacy): The terms, the refund policy, and the privacy notice are all published here, before the first sale.
- [Payments and Chargebacks](/domains/payments-and-chargebacks): Choosing a marketplace or a storefront decides who the [merchant of record](/glossary#merchant-of-record) is and what comes out of each sale, and each cut is on [this year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures#payments-and-chargebacks).
- [Email and Audience Rules](/domains/email-and-audience-rules): The signup form and the words beside it decide what each subscriber has agreed to receive.
