# This year's figures

This page is the one place on the site that prints figures set by law, regulation, or an agency: limits, rates, thresholds, and deadlines. It also holds fees set by a platform, and it is the only page on the site where a platform is named, and then only as the source of its own fee. The Business Side pages name each concept in plain language and link here, so a figure changes in one place.

Every value carries the year it applies to and a link to its primary source. A figure fixed by statute shows "Set by statute" in the Year column. A figure that differs from state to state shows "Varies by state" and points you to where to look. A fee or term set by a platform shows "Set by your platform's terms" and names the kind of page to check. Each figure appears once, under the area of The Business Side it fits best; if you do not see it under the area you came from, use your browser's find. The page is reviewed once a year.

Last checked: September 2026
